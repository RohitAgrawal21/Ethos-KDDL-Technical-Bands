3. The maths, on a day you pick

The 20 closes in the window
SMA_n = mean of the last n closes C (n = 20, 50, 200) % deviation_n = C / SMA_n - 1 (distance only; ignores volatility) SD_n = population standard deviation (divide by n) of the closing PRICES over the same n days z_n = (C - SMA_n) / SD_n (distance in standard deviations) Bollinger(20,2): Upper = SMA20 + 2*SD20, Lower = SMA20 - 2*SD20, %B = (C - Lower)/(Upper - Lower), bandwidth = (Upper - Lower)/SMA20
A close outside the bands is exactly |z20| ≥ 2. The bands are the z-score drawn on the price chart; %B and z20 carry the same information (%B = 0.5 + z20/4).
